Port Elin, on the tidal flats

Water remembers what the body forgets.

Selkie Bathhouse pairs cold-water therapy, saltwater soaking rooms, and slow, hands-on treatments in a bathhouse built into the harbor rock. Come for an hour. Stay for the tide.

Reserve a ritual See the menu

We built Selkie around one idea: the body softens fastest in water that changes temperature.

Every visit moves you through a circuit — warm room, cold plunge, steam, rest — the way harbor towns have bathed for generations, before it had a name like "contrast therapy." Nothing here is rushed. Most rituals end in a quiet room with tea and a view of the flats.

Selkie is not a quick-fix spa. We keep our roster of therapists small and our schedule unhurried, so a two-hour ritual actually takes two hours.
