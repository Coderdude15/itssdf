How it works

From first call to final nail

The same four steps whether it's a small repair or a full reroof.

01

Inspect & document

We walk the roof, photograph anything relevant, and check attic ventilation and flashing while we're up there.

02

Fixed-price quote

You get one written number covering materials, labor, and disposal — no change orders once work starts.

03

Install

Most residential reroofs are done in a single day. We protect your landscaping and run a magnetic nail sweep after.

04

Walkthrough & warranty

We walk the finished roof with you and register your manufacturer and workmanship warranties on the spot.

Materials

What we install

We'll walk you through trade-offs on cost, lifespan, and how each system handles this region's weather.

Architectural shingle

25–30 year lifespan. The most common choice for residential reroofs in this area.

Standing seam metal

40–50 year lifespan. Sheds snow and hail well; higher upfront cost.

Cedar shake

25–30 year lifespan. Common on older farmhouses and historic properties.

TPO / flat roofing

15–20 year lifespan. Built for additions, porches, and commercial low-slope roofs.
